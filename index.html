Why this exists

Most store documentation assumes you already know what to measure. It does not help with the failures that produce no metric at all: an order that was paid and never shipped still counts as revenue, a chargeback with two days left on the clock produces no notification, and a product page with no images is technically "published".

These three references are the checks that catch those. They are short because they are meant to be run, not read once.

Where to start

  1. Open the daily checklist and answer each line pass, fail or unknown. Unknown counts as fail.
  2. If a dispute is already open, go straight to the reason code map — the clock matters more than the checklist.
  3. If email stopped landing, the deliverability diagnosis names the three causes and rules them out in order.
If more than three boxes are unchecked, stop marketing and fix operations first. Traffic to a broken store only buys more complaints.
